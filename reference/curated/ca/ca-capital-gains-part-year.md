## Rules

### No Special Rate
- California taxes long- and short-term capital gains as regular income; no special rate exists. [Schedule CA (540NR) Instructions, Line 7a]

### Part-Year Residents
- Full-year nonresidents and part-year residents report gains and losses as if full-year residents for the entire year using California amounts, so all gains and losses are reported. Complete Schedule D (540NR) and the Schedule D (540NR) Worksheet for Nonresidents and Part-Year Residents to complete Schedule CA (540NR) column E. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Purpose]
- In the worksheet, column A is the amount as if a resident for the full year and column B is the amount as if a nonresident for the full year; the worksheet result (column E) is entered on the Part-Year Resident Worksheet, Section A, line 7a, column C, which carries to Schedule CA (540NR) column E. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet lines 4 and 5]
- Column E gain or loss is transactions while a resident (all sources) plus California-source transactions while a nonresident. Complete the worksheet columns taking into account the dates of the transactions. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet line 3; FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Capital Gains and Losses]
- For installment sales, gain on intangible property such as stock is generally sourced to the recipient's state of residence when the installment payment is received; real property is sourced to where it is located. [FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Installment Sales]
- Sourcing of a non-installment sale of stock by a part-year resident (before versus after the move): follow the transaction date split in the worksheet; any further sourcing rule: "I cannot verify this from provided FTB materials — check ftb.ca.gov".
- Installment proceeds received while a California resident are taxable by California even if the sale happened while a nonresident. [FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Installment Sales, Change of Residency to California]
- A brokerage summary total is not enough: the transaction dates are needed to split gains and losses by residency period. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet line 3]

### Carryovers
- Prior-year capital loss carryover is recalculated as if a resident for all prior years (column A) and as if a nonresident for all prior years (column B). Column C = column A x days resident / 365; column D = column B x days nonresident / 365 (366 in a leap year). [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet line 3; FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Capital Gains and Losses, Part-Year Resident]
- Use the Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet only if at year end you were a resident and column A line 4 shows a loss, or you were a nonresident and column B line 4 shows a loss; use the column A amounts if a resident at year end and the column B amounts if a nonresident. Full-year residents use a separate worksheet. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet]

### Basis Differences
- California basis can differ from federal basis (for example, assets with different depreciation, qualified small business stock, HSA investments). Use Schedule D (540NR) to adjust. [Schedule CA (540NR) Instructions, Line 7a; 2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Line 1 column (c)]
- Whether California wash-sale and basis-reporting treatment differs from federal: "I cannot verify this from provided FTB materials — check ftb.ca.gov".
