## Rules

### No Special Rate
- California taxes long- and short-term capital gains as regular income; no special rate exists. [Schedule CA (540NR) Instructions, Line 7a]

### Part-Year Residents
- Report gains and losses as if a full-year resident, using California amounts, for columns A-D. Complete Schedule D (540NR) and its Worksheet to fill column E. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, General Information]
- Column E gain or loss is transactions while a resident (all sources) plus California-source transactions while a nonresident. Complete the worksheet columns using the date of each transaction. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet; FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Capital Gains and Losses]
- Intangible property such as stock is generally sourced to the owner's state of residence at the time of sale; real property is sourced to where it is located. [FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Installment Sales]
- Installment proceeds received while a California resident are taxable by California even if the sale happened while a nonresident. [FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Installment Sales, Change of Residency to California]
- A brokerage summary total is not enough: the transaction dates are needed to split gains and losses by residency period.

### Carryovers
- Prior-year capital loss carryover is recalculated as if a resident for all prior years (column A) and as if a nonresident for all prior years (column B). Column C = column A x days resident / 365; column D = column B x days nonresident / 365 (366 in a leap year). [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions, Worksheet line 3; FTB Pub. 1100 (2025), Capital Gains and Losses, Part-Year Resident]
- Compute any carryover to 2026 with the Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet. [2025 Schedule D (540NR) Instructions]

### Basis Differences
- California basis can differ from federal basis (for example, assets with different depreciation, qualified small business stock, HSA investments). Use Schedule D (540NR) to adjust. [Schedule CA (540NR) Instructions, Line 7a]
- Wash sale and basis-reporting treatment follows the federal return unless a listed difference applies: "I cannot verify this from provided FTB materials — check ftb.ca.gov".
