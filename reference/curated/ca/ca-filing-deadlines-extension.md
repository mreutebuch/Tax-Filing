# California Filing Deadlines and Extension

## Rules

### Dates
- April 15, 2026: last day to file and pay the 2025 amount owed to avoid penalties and interest. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Important Dates]
- October 15, 2026: last day to file or e-file the 2025 return to avoid a late filing penalty and interest computed from April 15, 2026. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Important Dates]
- 100% of the amount owed must be paid by April 15, 2026 to avoid interest and penalties. Form FTB 3519 (Payment for Automatic Extension for Individuals) is the payment form; mail it with the payment or pay online at ftb.ca.gov/pay. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Important Dates; line 121 and 122 payment instructions]
- A payment sent with FTB 3519 is claimed on Form 540NR line 82. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Line 82]
- Whether a federal extension (Form 4868) changes any California requirement: "I cannot verify this from provided FTB materials — check ftb.ca.gov".
- If living or traveling outside the United States on April 15, 2026 the dates differ: see FTB 3519. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Important Dates]

### Late Filing
- A late filing penalty plus interest from the original due date applies if the return is filed after October 15, 2026; the maximum total penalty is 25% of the tax not paid. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Interest and Penalties]
- The minimum penalty for filing more than 60 days late is $135 or 100% of the balance due, whichever is less. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Interest and Penalties]
- Interest compounds daily and the rate is adjusted twice a year. [2025 Form 540NR Booklet, Interest and Penalties]
